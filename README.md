# peepoPog trade desk

**Open the site: https://bazbarian.github.io/peepopog-trade-desk/**

Dynasty trade dashboard for the peepoPog Sleeper league (superflex, PPR).

- Rosters, records and traded picks load live from the [Sleeper API](https://docs.sleeper.com/).
- Player and pick values come from [FantasyCalc](https://fantasycalc.com) (dynasty, superflex, PPR).
- Draft picks are re-valued by projected slot from the current standings.

## Using it

Enter your Sleeper username and click **Load league**, or open a league link someone shared. Then pick your team from the **Your team** dropdown. **Copy link to my view** gives you a bookmark that opens straight to your team.

Click **Load league** again any time to pick up new roster moves.

It's a single static page: no server, no keys, and everything runs in your browser.
